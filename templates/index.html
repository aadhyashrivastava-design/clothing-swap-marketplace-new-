How Bartering Combats Fast Fashion

1. Keeps Pre-Loved Garments in Rotation

Extending the life of a garment by just 9 months reduces its carbon, water, and waste footprints by around 20-30% each.

2. Eliminate Fast-Fashion Impulse Buys

Barter values the intrinsic craftsmanship and quality of materials (wool, raw denim, linen) over fleeting synthetic fast-fashion micro-trends.

3. Hyperlocal Zero-Emissions Swapping

Instead of air-shipping single items in non-recyclable plastic mailers, members swap in-person at neighborhood coffee shops and community library hubs.
